# Carrots in 3 Minutes

## 0:00 — FROM SEED TO SUPPER

Carrots in three minutes. Sweet, crunchy roots, straight from your own garden. Here's how to sow them, look after them, and bring in the harvest.

## 0:12 — 01 / PREPARE

Choose a sunny spot with loose, well-drained soil. Remove stones and break up hard lumps. Skip fresh manure, which can encourage forked roots. On heavy or shallow ground, choose a short-rooted variety.

## 0:26 — 02 / WHEN TO SOW

For a straightforward UK crop, sow from April to June, once the soil is workable and warming. Early varieties can go under cover in March. Sow another small batch every few weeks for a longer harvest.

## 0:38 — 03 / SOW DIRECT

Water a shallow drill, then scatter seed thinly, about one centimetre deep. Leave fifteen to thirty centimetres between rows. Cover lightly and firm gently. Sow direct: transplanting can damage the developing root.

## 0:52 — 04 / GET THEM STARTED

Germination can take around two to three weeks, longer in cold soil. Keep the seed zone moist, without waterlogging it. Watch for slugs while seedlings are small, and remove weeds before they take over.

## 1:06 — 05 / MAKE ROOM

Thin only crowded seedlings, leaving five to eight centimetres between plants. Work gently in damp conditions, remove the thinnings, and firm the soil around the plants you keep. Avoid bruising the foliage.

## 1:18 — 06 / LOOK AFTER THEM

Once established, water deeply during prolonged dry spells. Pots need checking more often. A drought followed by a sudden soaking can split roots. Weed carefully by hand close to the carrots, keeping disturbance to a minimum.

## 1:33 — 07 / PROTECT THE CROP

Carrot fly larvae tunnel into the roots. Cover the whole crop with fine insect mesh, securing every edge. Replace it straight after weeding or thinning, and avoid ground where carrots were recently grown.

## 1:47 — 08 / HARVEST

Harvest when the roots are big enough to eat, usually twelve to sixteen weeks after sowing. Baby carrots may be ready around eight weeks. Start at one end of the row; loosen hard soil with a fork before pulling.

## 2:00 — 09 / ENJOY & STORE

Enjoy some freshly pulled. For storage, lift before hard frost or prolonged wet weather. Trim the leaves and pack sound roots in sand in a cool, airy place. Check regularly and remove any that rot.
