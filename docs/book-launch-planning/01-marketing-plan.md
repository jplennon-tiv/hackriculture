# The Vegetable Guru online marketing plan

19 October 2026. Proposal for John Lennon, Hackriculture.

Use the existing YouTube relationship to demonstrate the book’s usefulness, send viewers to one clear book page on hackriculture.com, then hand ready buyers to Bookvault’s hosted shop. Keep the launch small enough to run alongside normal gardening videos. The first physical proof is ordered; publication, price and the purchase link remain pending.

## Positioning and readers

The strongest promise is already the subtitle: **Your at-a-glance growing companion**. The book brings 44 vegetable guides, 14 crop-group Troubles guides and practical Growing Techniques into one illustrated reference. Its value is finding the next useful action: when to sow, how to plant, what a damaged leaf suggests, or how to plan a bed. [Current book scope and production status](../../../hackriculture-print/docs/handover/START-HERE.md).

Prioritise existing UK gardening viewers who trust John and want a reference beside them when planning and growing. Next come beginners wanting a manageable first plot and experienced growers wanting a quick reminder. Gardeners’ gift buyers are a secondary audience once the physical quality and delivery arrangements are established. These are proposed audiences, not measured customer segments.

The current proof is a 185 × 240 mm, 168-page full-colour paperback using imperial measurements. Calendar guidance centres on lowland England, with conditions and crop advice qualifying timings. Show those details plainly; do not suggest universal climate coverage, a metric edition, waterproof pages or lay-flat binding. The physical proof must establish readability, colour and opening comfort before final sales photography.

## Why buy when the website is free

Keep all approved free growing guides useful and accessible. Videos demonstrate the work and show John’s judgement; the website answers immediate questions; the book collects organised crop spreads, illustrated diagnosis, techniques and an index in a physical object that can be consulted repeatedly. Sell this convenience and presentation without claiming that gardening knowledge is exclusive to the book.

The approved introduction encourages starting with one useful patch. Techniques pages emphasise observing conditions, keeping records and adapting methods. Carry that practical tone into promotion: show a gardening decision and the page that helps with it. Avoid guaranteed yields or savings claims. [Current reading preview](../../../hackriculture-print/output/pdf/whole-book-order-preview/the-vegetable-guru-order-preview.pdf).

Offer an ungated sample with readable page images and a downloadable extract. Recommended choices for John’s selection are Beetroot, pages 12–13; Bean and Pea Troubles, pages 100–101, clearly labelled as an extract from a longer section; and Rotation & planning, pages 148–149. They demonstrate the three distinct parts of the book. Retain complete page context and explain the calendar key alongside the sample. These are current printed folios, not PDF viewer numbers.

## The route from viewing to buying

Use one memorable destination, proposed as **hackriculture.com/book/**, in relevant video descriptions, spoken mentions and channel links. Its first screen should identify John, show the real book, state who it helps and offer “Look inside” alongside the purchase action. Continue with the three kinds of help, readable samples, format details and concise buying answers.

Bookvault’s official [Buy Now guide](https://help.bookvault.app/how-to-create-buy-now-links-in-bookvault) confirms that a generated link puts the selected title directly into the **Bookvault Shop basket**. Use that link once tested. Label the button with the buyer-facing shop name, and explain that payment and delivery continue there. Retain a product-page link for visitors wanting retailer details. Do not construct a URL from the internal title number.

The [Shop introduction](https://bookvault.app/introducing-bookvault-shop/) connects activation to distribution settings for Bookvault Shop and The Great British Bookshop, and supports supplementary listing images. Their names are not sufficient evidence that all fees and checkout behaviour are identical. The exact live destination, compensation, delivery countries, postage, dispatch estimate, returns and order-help route need checking on this title before launch. The author’s proof postage is not a customer delivery quotation.

The working £17.50 price is not approved or saved as a retail price. [Official guidance](https://help.bookvault.app/book-trade-discount) gives The Great British Bookshop a minimum 20% trade discount. Bookvault’s separate [no-commission guidance](https://help.bookvault.app/fees-commission-selling-direct) concerns own-store or manual fulfilment; do not apply it to the hosted shop. Confirm the actual channel’s publisher compensation before promotions. Settle metadata and the deferred ISBN decision before activating sales; [Bookvault distinguishes internal identifiers from registered ISBNs](https://help.bookvault.app/does-bookvault-provide-a-free-isbn).

## Launch in three phases

**While the proof is pending:** prepare the book page, samples and questions; show “Coming soon” with “Look inside”, without an active purchase button. Record a short explanation of why John made the book. Ask viewers which crop or recurring problem they most often look up. Use their replies to choose demonstrations. Do not promise a release day yet.

**When publication and checkout are ready:** film one practical launch video using the actual copy. Demonstrate finding a sowing instruction, diagnosing a problem and planning the next crop. Add one concise book invitation after useful advice. Publish a community announcement and update a small selection of relevant older video descriptions. Test the whole mobile journey through the final payable total, with any test purchase made separately by John. Announce availability only after the live listing works.

**For the following six to eight weeks:** continue normal advice videos, selecting topics that naturally demonstrate a book feature. Repurpose useful moments into short clips. Answer recurring purchase questions on the book page and use genuine reader feedback only with permission. If release falls in winter, emphasise plot planning and next season’s choices; introduce seasonal crop demonstrations as relevant. Gift promotion depends on verified delivery dates.

## Budget and workload

Start with **£0 paid advertising** and no extra shop subscription. Use existing filming equipment, website hosting and approved artwork. Hold an optional **£50–£100** reserve for a small number of review copies and postage after checking actual costs; this is a proposed ceiling, not a purchase instruction. Avoid blanket discounting until the margin is known.

Allow roughly two additional hours a week for the first six weeks: one hour preparing a book demonstration from existing filming, half an hour improving links and page answers, and half an hour reviewing results. If that is burdensome, reduce posting frequency rather than multiplying platforms. An email list can follow if John wants to maintain one; sample access should not depend on it.

## Outcomes and review

The first outcome is a reliable buying journey: every promoted link works, samples are readable on a phone, and the customer sees an accurate format, price and delivery explanation before ordering.

Review weekly: visits to the book page by campaign, sample use, outbound purchase clicks, recorded book sales and publisher compensation. Site measurement needs implementing separately; the existing site has no analytics. Give each video a distinct campaign link, but count a Bookvault click as purchase intent, not a sale. [Bookvault reporting](https://help.bookvault.app/how-do-i-know-who-has-bought-my-book) provides title sales and compensation; it does not establish which video caused each purchase or guarantee access to buyer emails.

After four weeks, compare each promotion with its own audience exposure. Low visits suggest improving the invitation; visits without purchase clicks suggest improving proof and clarity; clicks without corresponding sales suggest reviewing the external journey and delivered price. Set sales targets after observing this baseline rather than inventing a conversion forecast.
