# Hackriculture website goals for The Vegetable Guru launch

19 October 2026. Proposed direction for John Lennon.

Make hackriculture.com the place where YouTube viewers recognise John, find the help promised in a video, inspect The Vegetable Guru and decide whether to buy it. The website should explain the book and prepare the buyer; Bookvault’s hosted shop should handle the order. Keep the existing free growing guides useful and easy to find.

## How the two plans shape the website

The [marketing plan](01-marketing-plan.md) centres on the book’s convenience as an illustrated physical reference. The website must demonstrate that value through real, readable spreads and practical examples, alongside an honest description of format and scope. Its proposed single `/book/` destination gives announcements and older video links somewhere stable to point.

The [YouTube plan](02-youtube-content-plan.md) centres on solving a crop problem, recognising trouble or deciding what to do next. The website should continue the particular subject viewers were watching. It should also support the existing 101 Gardening Tips format without requiring a separate publishing programme.

The current [homepage](https://hackriculture.com/) offers guides, selected videos and John’s story, then encourages a return to YouTube. The [growing index](https://hackriculture.com/growing/) already provides a substantial free entry point. Add the missing book journey while retaining that trust and usefulness.

## The visitor journeys

| Visitor’s immediate purpose | Recommended route |
| --- | --- |
| Saw a book announcement or physical demonstration | Video → `/book/` → relevant samples and buying answers → Bookvault |
| Wants help with the crop shown in a video | Video → existing crop guide → related book example → `/book/` buying section → Bookvault |
| Wants the plot planning or compost example | Video → relevant section within `/book/` → readable technique sample → Bookvault |
| Arrives through search or browses the channel website | Guide, homepage or video catalogue → clear book invitation → `/book/` |

Give each promoted video one primary destination. The launch destination is the book page; practical crop demonstrations can lead directly to the existing guide. Shorts need a profile link or eligible related full video because their description and comment URLs are not clickable, as detailed in the YouTube report.

## Five website goals

**1. Make the book immediately recognisable.** Add “The book” to the main navigation and introduce it prominently on the homepage. Show the title, John’s name, an approved cover or physical photograph, who it helps and current availability. Keep “Look inside” available from the first screen. An existing follower should understand the connection between Hackriculture and the book without reading the About page.

**2. Make the value visible before asking for a purchase.** Organise the explanation around growing a crop, identifying trouble and planning or caring for the plot. Show representative crop, Troubles and technique pages with sufficient resolution to read on a phone, plus an optional extract download. Explain the calendar key. The marketing report proposes Beetroot, a clearly labelled Bean and Pea Troubles extract, and Rotation & planning for John’s selection. Keep access ungated.

**3. Preserve useful free entry points.** Retain `/growing/`, all 44 approved crop URLs, `/videos/` and `/about/`. Add relevant book invitations after useful content without rewriting approved gardening prose. Pair selected videos with appropriate guides where the match is verified. Replace the homepage’s dated “Fresh from” framing with an honest selection label, and prioritise relevant gardening films. Keep food, DIY and energy material available in the video catalogue.

**4. Give ready buyers a clear, truthful handoff.** State the confirmed edition, measurement units, page count, size and paperback format. Explain the UK growing context. Show the confirmed price and whether delivery is additional; link to the retailer’s current delivery and order-help information. Label purchase buttons with the actual shop name and explain that payment and delivery continue there. Verify that the generated link opens the correct title or basket, including on a phone. Do not construct a link from an internal stock number.

**5. Keep the launch manageable.** Use five primary destinations: Home, The book, Growing guides, Videos and About John. Put samples and buying FAQs within the book page, with direct section links. Add only necessary footer information, including accurate privacy information for any selected services. Keep availability, price and purchase destination maintained together so repeated invitations remain consistent. A newsletter, customer account system and website checkout are outside the initial build.

## Before and after sales open

While the physical proof is pending, show “Coming soon” and make “Look inside” the main action. Label provisional preview imagery appropriately. Keep the working £17.50 price and unconfirmed release date out of public sales promises. A secondary YouTube follow link can maintain contact without creating a mailing service.

Activate purchase buttons after the proof and publication decisions are settled and the public listing, price, delivered cost and support route have been checked. The [current handover](../../../hackriculture-print/docs/handover/START-HERE.md) records that no live purchase URL exists. If availability changes later, update the message and retain the samples; every page should reflect the same sales state.

## Measurement and acceptance

The business outcome is paid book sales with worthwhile publisher compensation. Website handoffs are a leading indicator. Record campaign arrivals, book-page visits, sample use and outbound shop clicks, then review actual title sales separately. Never report a shop click as a sale or assign a retailer order to a video without supporting attribution.

Choose measurement that the static site and budget can support. The current site has no analytics. If a small analytics service is added, report raw counts alongside rates and their denominators, and avoid counting repeated clicks as separate buyers. If the site remains tracker-free, use available hosting page counts and retailer sales, accepting that the complete journey cannot be measured. Campaign tags alone do not collect results.

Before launch, confirm:

- Every existing guide URL still works and approved advice is preserved.
- Samples are readable on mobile, images have useful descriptions, and navigation and buttons work with a keyboard.
- Book-page purchase actions reach the verified external destination in one click and clearly identify the seller.
- Displayed specifications, availability and price match the actual edition and listing.
- John can update launch status and the purchase link without revising the guide collection.

Review the first four weeks as a baseline, then compare later promotions at similar ages. Improve the invitation when few viewers arrive, the book explanation when visitors do not continue, and the retailer journey when purchase interest does not correspond with orders. Set numerical sales targets once those observations exist.
