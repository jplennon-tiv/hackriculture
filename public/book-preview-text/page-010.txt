Jerusalem artichokes are not true artichokes at all, but vigorous sunflower relatives grown for their knobbly
underground tubers. They are hardy, reliable and useful for winter soups, stews and grated salads, with a sweet,
nutty flavour which is not to everybody's taste and may not suit every digestion. The plants grow very tall in rich
soil, making them useful as a screen or windbreak but also capable of robbing light from lower crops if planted
in a long row. They are easy to grow from tubers, but hard to eradicate once established because even small
pieces left in the soil can regrow.
