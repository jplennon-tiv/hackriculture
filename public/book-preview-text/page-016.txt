Celeriac, or turnip-rooted celery, is grown for its swollen stem-base. Its earthy celery flavour suits soups, stews,
salads and winter dishes; lifted roots keep into spring if stored cool. It needs less blanching and earthing-up
work than celery, but still needs a full season, a good start under cover and rich, moisture-retentive soil.
Shallow, fibrous roots suffer in dry ground: water steadily in dry weather. Heavy, well-composted soil suits it
well; varieties are broadly similar.
