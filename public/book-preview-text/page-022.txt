Potatoes give heavy crops from small plots; check old grassland for wireworms. Plant seed tubers, not true
seed, and keep new tubers covered: light makes them green and poisonous. Eat first/second earlies fresh.
Maincrops yield and store better, but need more space and face more blight. Give rich soil and moisture; guard
frost-tender leaves. Lift gently; store maincrops cool, dark and frost-free.
