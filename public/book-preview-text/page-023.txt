Pests & diseases
Problem                       What to look for                What to do
BLIGHT                        Brown collapsing foliage;       Remove affected haulm; allow skins to set before lifting
                              tubers may rot.                 where possible.
SLUGS                         Deep tunnels spoil tubers.      Avoid slug refuges; lift mature crops promptly.
SCAB                          Corky skin patches on tubers.   Avoid liming; keep soil moist as tubers form.
EELWORM                       Stunted patches; cysts on       Rotate; use suitable resistant varieties after diagnosis.
                              roots.
FROST                         Young foliage blackens.         Cover shoots before frost; unfrozen plants may regrow.
STORAGE ROTS                  Tubers soften or decay in       Store only sound dry tubers; inspect and remove rot.
                              store.

                              wilt.
