Fast-growing radishes provide mild, juicy spring roots and suit spare corners, window boxes, protected sowings
or gaps between slower crops. Pick small red or red-white types young: heat, age and drought make them hot,
woody, hollow or soft. Longer-growing winter radishes come in white, pink, black or red-white. Grate, pickle,
braise or stir-fry them; autumn-lifted roots also store in cool, damp conditions.
