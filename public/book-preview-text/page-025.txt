Pests & diseases
Problem                         What to look for                    What to do
FLEA BEETLE                     Shot-holed leaves.                  Keep moist; use fine mesh.
BOLTING                         Early flowers; poor roots.          Sow winter radishes from July onwards.
CABBAGE ROOT FLY                Roots tunnelled by maggots.         Use mesh; trim minor root damage.
WOODY OR HOLLOW                 Hot, woody or hollow roots.         Water evenly; thin and harvest promptly.
