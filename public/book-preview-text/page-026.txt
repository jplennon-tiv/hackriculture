Salsify and scorzonera are related but distinct root crops, grown for delicacy rather than bulk. Thin, brittle roots
are awkward to lift and clean, so usable yields may be modest. Creamy-rooted salsify, the “vegetable oyster”,
has a delicate oyster-like flavour; scorzonera is black-skinned. Both need deep, friable soil and direct sowing,
and withstand most winter weather. Roots left for a second season may flower attractively; young flower stalks
are edible before they toughen.
