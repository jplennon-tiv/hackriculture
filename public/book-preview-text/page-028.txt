Swedes are hardy brassica roots: slower, sweeter, more substantial and much more frost-resistant than turnips.
Orange or yellow flesh and leaves rising from a neck help distinguish them. Cold improves their flavour for
boiling, mashing and seasoning. Allow about a month longer than turnips, with room for roots to swell. June
sowings can follow early lettuce, carrots, broad beans or other early crops.
