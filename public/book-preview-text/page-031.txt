Pests & diseases
Problem                        What to look for                 What to do
FLEA BEETLE                    Tiny holes in young leaves.      Mesh seedlings; water to keep growth steady.
CABBAGE ROOT FLY               Maggots tunnel roots.            Cover after sowing; discard badly damaged roots.
WOODY ROOTS                    Hard, fibrous or strong roots.   Keep growth steady; pull spring roots young.
BORON DEFICIENCY               Grey-brown or hollow cores.      Confirm with a soil test; seek organic advice, never
                                                                overdose.
