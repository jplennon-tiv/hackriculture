Broccoli includes summer and autumn calabrese, with a central green head followed by side shoots, and purple
or white sprouting types, which give small spears through late winter and spring when other greens are scarce.
Garden heads may have more blemishes than shop-bought ones; protect summer crops from caterpillars and
pigeons. Match variety to harvest: early calabrese for summer, late calabrese for autumn, and sprouting types for
late winter and spring. The flavour and long season reward the effort.
