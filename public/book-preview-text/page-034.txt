Brussels sprouts form cabbage-like buds up a tall stem, ripening through autumn and winter. Freshly picked
buttons have a sweet, nutty flavour; small, firm ones are good raw or cooked. Firm, fertile soil, careful
transplanting and steady growth are the keys. Variety choice, poor soil or checked growth can produce open,
leafy sprouts. Pick regularly and remove yellow leaves to keep the crop clean and usable from October into
March.
