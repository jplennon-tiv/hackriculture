Cabbages can feed you for much of the year if you match varieties to their seasons. Spring types give tender
greens and small heads; summer and autumn types provide fresh heads for salads, slaws and cooking. Winter,
savoy and red cabbages can stand or store into colder months. Types differ in shape, texture, colour, hardiness
and speed of hearting.
