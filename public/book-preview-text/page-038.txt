Cauliflower needs uninterrupted growth for tight heads of flower buds. Poor soil, loose planting, drought,
crowding or transplant checks can cause button heads or blind plants. Choose seasonal or mini types; give rich,
firm soil, steady moisture and space. Cut curds before they loosen or buds open.
