Kale, also known as borecole, is a hardy, non-hearting brassica grown for its leaves and young spring shoots. It
used to be treated as a plain winter standby, but modern varieties give a wide range of colours, textures and
flavours, from deeply curled green leaves to red and flat-leaved types. It is one of the most dependable crops for
autumn, winter and early spring, often standing when little else is available, and the flavour is generally better
after frost.
