Dependable broad beans are hardier than peas and tolerate heavier soil than many beans. Autumn sowings can
overwinter for an early harvest; spring sowings give the main summer crop. Seeds may make several square-
stemmed shoots, with scented black-and-white flowers and large pods of green, white or reddish beans. Pick
young for sweetness; mature beans become starchier but can be dried for winter food.
