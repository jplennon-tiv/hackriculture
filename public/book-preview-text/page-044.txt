French beans give crisp young pods, fresh shelled flageolets or dried haricots. Much less hardy than broad
beans, they need warm soil but then grow quickly and crop generously. Dwarfs crop first and need no formal
supports; low pods risk mud splash and slugs. Climbers need a frame, but keep pods off the ground, reduce
bending and crop longer. Pods can be round or flat, green, yellow or purple; purple ones usually turn green
when cooked.
