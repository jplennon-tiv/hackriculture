Runner beans are vigorous, ornamental climbers with red, white, pink or bicoloured flowers and long, flat pods.
They can crop heavily from late summer into autumn and make a useful screen or colourful wigwam. Less
drought-tolerant than French beans, they need rich, moisture-retentive ground and flourish in mild, moist
summers. Prepare generously, give strong supports, water well in hot, dry spells and pick young pods every few
days. Older stringed varieties need more kitchen preparation than modern stringless types.
