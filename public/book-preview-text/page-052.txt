Chicory is grown in two useful ways: for green salad leaves in summer and autumn, and for pale, crisp chicons
forced indoors to give fresh winter salad. The flavour is refreshing, tart and sometimes bitter, so it divides
opinion, and excluding light produces paler, milder chicons. Forcing types such as Witloof are raised for their
long roots, lifted in late autumn and brought into growth in dark, cool conditions; non-forcing types make larger
lettuce-like heads for autumn use. The crop is generally healthy and not difficult, although forcing takes extra
work and the roots are easiest to handle when grown in deep, loose soil.
