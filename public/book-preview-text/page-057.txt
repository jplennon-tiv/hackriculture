Pests & diseases
Problem                         What to look for                What to do
SLUGS                           Ragged leaves; slime trails.    Hand-pick after dark; protect seedlings.
ROOT APHID                      Wilting; white patches on       Water well; choose root-aphid resistance.
                                roots.
Bolting                         Flower stems; bitter leaves.    Water steadily; sow in season; pick promptly.
Downy Mildew                    Yellow patches; white mould     Remove affected leaves; space and ventilate plants.
                                beneath.
Aphid                           Sticky, curled leaves;          Squash colonies; encourage predators.
                                colonies.
Grey Mould (Botrytis)           Grey mould; rotting stems.      Remove diseased plants; clear debris; ventilate.
Cutworm                         Stems severed at soil level.    Search beside cut stems; remove caterpillars.
TIPBURN                         Brown, scorched leaf edges.     Keep moisture steady; remove decaying leaves.
No Hearts                       Hearting varieties stay open.   Check variety and maturity; space and water well.
