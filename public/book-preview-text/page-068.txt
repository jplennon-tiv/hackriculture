Aubergines are decorative, heat-loving relatives of the tomato, grown for their glossy fruits as well as their
purple flowers and large velvety leaves. They can crop well under glass or in a warm polytunnel, but outdoors
they are a gamble except in very warm, sheltered places. The key requirement is heat: cool, grey weather slows
growth and often delays fruiting until late summer. Treat aubergines as tender annuals, give them rich soil,
steady moisture without waterlogging, and harvest the fruits while the skin still has its shine. Fruits may be oval,
round or long, and varieties range from the familiar dark purple to white, violet and striped forms.
