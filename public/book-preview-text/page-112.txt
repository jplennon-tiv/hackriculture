Carrot and Parsnip                                                                                    Troubles

Carrots and parsnips share several root pests and diseases. Carrots are especially vulnerable to carrot fly;
parsnips also suffer from old seed and canker. Start with fresh seed, deep well-prepared soil and a clean
seedbed. Avoid fresh manure; use resistant varieties where available.
