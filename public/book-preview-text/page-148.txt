Organic growing Rotation & planning
Rotation means moving plant families to different ground instead of growing the same or similar
crops in one place year after year. It has two useful jobs: reducing shared pests and diseases, and
helping leafy crops use nitrogen left by peas and beans.

   A rotation is not a disease cure
   Clubroot and onion white rot, to take the two worst examples, can remain in soil for many years. A short
   circuit round the beds does not make infected ground clean. Keep contaminated soil off clean beds, and
   do not put infected allium debris into good compost.
